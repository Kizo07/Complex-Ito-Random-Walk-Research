# Phase 8 — Literature review and novelty audit: Fourier/COS pricing under stochastic correlation

**Date:** 2026-08-21
**Status:** web-verified re-audit for Phase 8 scope. Snippet/abstract-level
unless marked ✔.

---

## 1. What this phase adds

Phase 6–7 established exact laws for *barrier/correlation-level* payoffs via
the DDS clock. Phase 8 attacks **European vanilla spread/basket options**:
the payoff depends on \(Z_T=\alpha_1F_1+\alpha_2F_2\), which is
*conditionally Gaussian* given the correlation path with clock
\(v=\beta T+\alpha A_T\). Three new objects:

1. **Exact one-dimensional characteristic function**
   \(\phi_Z(u)=e^{iuz_0-u^2\beta T/2}L_A(\alpha u^2/2)\) with
   \(L_A(s)=\mathbb E[e^{-sA_T}]\) a *real-potential* forward-Feynman–Kac
   resolvent. Boundedness of the factor makes \(A_T\) supported on
   \((-T,T)\), so \(L_A\) exists for every real \(s\) — the bounded-factor
   structure is exactly what makes the transform globally defined.
2. **COS pricing** (Fang–Oosterlee 2008 ✔, SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 31:826–848)
   from that characteristic function: deterministic European prices with
   spectral convergence in mode count, no joint characteristic function and
   no tensor-product dimension growth.
3. **Implied-correlation smiles**: inverting constant-correlation Bachelier
   quotes against the stochastic-correlation price. The model's falsifiable
   signature: spread and basket implied-correlation curves are symmetric in
   moneyness with **opposite curvature** (wings down for spreads, up for
   baskets), extending the Milestone-3 sign corollary from barriers to full
   smile geometry.

## 2. Prior art found

### 2.1 Multi-asset Fourier/COS pricing (the machinery we consume)

- **Fang & Oosterlee (2008)**: 1-D COS method ✔ (original paper abstract).
- **Ruijter & Oosterlee (2012, SISC 34)**: 2-D COS for rainbow/basket
  options ✔ (CWI PDF). Requires the *bivariate* characteristic function;
  coefficient tensors grow exponentially with dimension; applied to Lévy/GBM
  baskets — no stochastic-correlation model.
- **COS–TT–CHF (arXiv 2608.17636, 2026)** ✔: state-of-the-art tensor-train
  COS for multi-asset Lévy/affine models up to d=30; explicitly framed as
  attacking the curse of dimensionality of the *joint* characteristic
  function. Model classes: GBM, VG, NIG, common-Heston — no stochastic
  correlation between assets.
- **COS-CPD (Brands, TU Delft thesis)** ✔: canonical-polyadic decomposition
  of the joint COS coefficient tensor; same joint-CF requirement.
- **Caldana–Fusai-type spread-option formulas and extensions (2013–2016)** ✔:
  lower-bound/approximation formulas requiring the joint CF of log-returns;
  lognormal or Lévy marginals; no stochastic correlation.

### 2.2 Pricing under stochastic correlation

- Phase 6 audit stands: Teng–Ehrhardt–Günther-type models price quantos by
  conditional Monte Carlo around Black–Scholes; no exact transform results
  for path-dependent or multi-asset payoffs.
- New searches ("Fourier pricing stochastic correlation", "characteristic
  function spread option stochastic correlation", "implied correlation
  smile stochastic correlation model", 2024–2026 window): returns only
  (i) local-correlation / pairwise-correlation smile literature priced by
  Monte Carlo or PDE (Langnau; Ahdida–Alfonsi; Brockhaus–Zetocha lineage),
  (ii) Wishart-based multi-curve models with swaption approximations
  (da Fonseca et al.), (iii) Heston-style stochastic-*volatility* COS
  pricing (not correlation). **No published exact characteristic function
  for any stochastic-correlation model was found**, and no published
  implied-correlation smile generated by an exactly-priced stochastic-
  correlation model.

## 3. Novelty boundary

**Cannot be claimed:** the COS method itself; multidimensional COS; the
conditional-Gaussianity observation (classical, e.g. in stochastic-vol
mixtures); Jensen bounds on variance mixtures.

**Can be claimed (after verification below):**
1. The first exact, globally defined characteristic function of a
   spread/basket return under a stochastic-correlation model, obtained by
   reducing the correlation factor to a bounded occupation integral whose
   Laplace transform is a real-potential FK resolvent.
2. Deterministic European pricing (COS or clock-density quadrature) where
   the audited literature has only Monte Carlo/approximations.
3. The opposite-curvature implied-correlation smile signature and its
   explanation (variance-mixture tails raise wing implied variance, pushing
   spread implied correlation down and basket implied correlation up).
4. Semi-analytic Greeks from the clock density (delta/gamma/vegas as
   one-dimensional integrals against the Milestone-4 density).
