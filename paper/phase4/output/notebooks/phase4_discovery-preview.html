This notebook records the constructive and computational route to

[ _t=_0 !( t +(+i)W_t ). ]

We begin with desired log-radius and phase dynamics, reconcile the ordinary exponential with the complex Itô SDE, and then test the pathwise identities, transition laws, moments, covariance rank, Euler–Maruyama convergence, and quadratic variations. The notebook generates every figure and numerical table used by the Phase 4 paper. Analytic claims are proved in the manuscript; seeded simulations are diagnostics, not proofs.

def assert_inside_phase4(path: Path) -> Path:
    '''Reject output paths escaping the Phase 4 paper root.'''
    resolved = path.resolve()

def save_figure(fig: plt.Figure, stem: str, alt_text: str) -> None:
    '''Save vector PDF and raster PNG companions under paper/phase4.'''
    if not stem.startswith("f") or len(alt_text.strip()) < 20:
        raise ValueError("figure stems and descriptive alt text are required")
    vector_path = assert_inside_phase4(VECTOR_DIR / f"{stem}.pdf")
    raster_path = assert_inside_phase4(RASTER_DIR / f"{stem}.png")
    fig.savefig(vector_path)
    fig.savefig(raster_path, dpi=300)
    FIGURE_ROWS.append(
        {
            "stem": stem,
            "vector": vector_path.relative_to(PAPER_ROOT).as_posix(),
            "raster": raster_path.relative_to(PAPER_ROOT).as_posix(),
            "alt_text": alt_text.strip(),
        }
    )
    plt.show()
    plt.close(fig)


def write_csv(name: str, frame: pd.DataFrame) -> None:

def write_json(name: str, payload: dict[str, object]) -> None:
    '''Write sorted, indented JSON without nonfinite values.'''

        raise ValueError("JSON output name must end in .json")
    encoded = json.dumps(payload, indent=2, sort_keys=True, allow_nan=False)
    path.write_text(encoded + "\n", encoding="utf-8")

1. Requirements and coefficient reconciliation

Prescribe a real GBM modulus and a continuous phase lift:

[ =,dt+,dW_t, d_t=,dt+,dW_t. ]

The direct complex log increment is

[ ,dt+B,dW_t, =(-^2)+i, B=+i. ]

For (Z_t=Z_0e^{t+BW_t}), Itô’s formula gives (dZ_t/Z_t=A,dt+B,dW_t), where

[ A=+12B^2 =-^2+i(+). ]

The complex square (B^2), not (|B|^2), supplies the correction.

2. Pathwise modulus, phase, and exactness

Taking real and imaginary parts of the exponent gives

[ R_t=R_0e{(-2/2)t+W_t}, _t=_0+t+W_t. ]

Thus (R) is exactly the prescribed real GBM, while the imaginary exponent is a continuous unwrapped phase. The complex exponential never reaches zero when (Z_0), so this phase lift is available for every finite time on each path.

3. Exact multiplicative walk and transition geometry

For a finite step (h) and Brownian increment (W_nN(0,h)),

[ _{n+1} =_n !(h+BW_n). ]

This is the requested random-walk formula: the real and imaginary parts of one complex log increment combine scale and rotation. At a fixed horizon, log-radius and phase are jointly Gaussian but are driven by one scalar normal variable, so their covariance is rank one and their support is a line in log-polar space.

7. Euler–Maruyama and complex quadratic variation

Euler–Maruyama applied to

[ d_t=A_t,dt+B_t,dW_t ]

has strong order (1/2) in the usual globally Lipschitz linear setting. Shared Brownian paths isolate discretization error across levels.

Two complex brackets carry different information:

[ d[,]_t=B2_t2,dt, d[,]_t =|B|2|_t|2,dt. ]

The first is bilinear and complex; the second is Hermitian, nonnegative, and measures total planar quadratic variation.
